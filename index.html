About

A varied path, built on fieldwork

Utkarsh's work spans government policy evaluation, cognitive science research, and human-AI interaction design. His experience is varied by design — he has run large surveys in rural India, built computational models of human behavior, and studied how people use AI tools in everyday settings.

Much of his work involves direct site visits: meeting communities, training field teams, and collecting data in person rather than only from a desk. He currently works as a Research Associate at IIM Ahmedabad and holds an M.Sc. in Cognitive Science from IIT Delhi.

Projects

Field studies, models, and user research

Field Study · IIM Ahmedabad, with Ministry of Rural Development

Rural Development Programme Evaluation

Site visits — Rajasthan, Delhi & two other states

Utkarsh helped design and run a large evaluation of a government rural development programme. He built the survey, then travelled to three states for data collection, visiting villages and working directly with local teams. He hired and trained field teams of over 30 people in each district.

Over 6,000 people were surveyed in person. Back in Delhi, he analyzed the data in R and Python and co-wrote the final report, which was presented to the Ministry.

Field visits Survey research Government evaluation

My role: I helped design the evaluation study, built the survey instruments, and managed data collection on the ground.

My experience: This was my first time running research at this scale. Coordinating field teams across three states taught me how much groundwork a good survey needs before a single response comes in.

Field Study · IIM Ahmedabad, EY & Gujarat Sports Infrastructure Development

Ex-Ante Impact Assesment for India's Olympic Bid 2036

Site visits — Gujarat, government meetings

Utkarsh worked on a large public perception survey of about 6,000 people to support India's official Olympic bid. This involved site visits and meetings with government officials in Gujarat.

He also co-wrote the environmental sustainability plan for the bid, comparing it against international standards. The findings were presented directly to senior government officials and used in India's official bid.

Field visits Public policy Sustainability research

My role: I worked on both the survey side and the environmental sustainability framework, moving between primary data collection and policy writing.

My experience: Presenting findings directly to senior government officials showed me how research needs to be framed differently for policymakers than for an academic audience.

Research Project

AI Assistance and Task Performance

I studied how people's trust in AI assistance affects how well they perform tasks. I used Bayesian modelling in Python to understand differences between individuals. The work was accepted for a poster presentation at a cognitive science conference in 2026.

AI research Statistical modelling

My role: I led the statistical re-analysis independently, and produced results

My experience: Running a fully remote research collaboration across time zones taught me to communicate findings clearly and work asynchronously without losing momentum.

Applied Research · Class Project

Usability and UX Research for Zomato

In-person usability testing

I studied how people choose and book restaurants using in-person usability testing. His recommendations led to an 18% drop in task time and a 15% rise in task success.

User research Usability testing

My role: I designed and ran the usability tests, then worked with designers to test alternative prototypes.

My experience: Seeing a measurable improvement in task completion after our changes made the value of usability testing very concrete for me — the numbers backed up what we'd heard in sessions.

Master's Thesis · IIT Delhi

Human-AI Interaction in Digital Learning

For his thesis, Utkarsh studied how AI can improve digital learning. He built an AI system that reads both text and audio to recognize emotions during learning, and used a fine-tuned language model to generate easier-to-read learning content automatically.

AI research Education Machine learning

My role: I designed and built the full pipeline myself, from the emotion-recognition model to the content-generation system.

My experience: This was the most technical project I've taken on. It pushed me to learn multimodal machine learning largely on my own, and to make it work end to end.

Research Internship (Remote)· King's College London

Pain Perception Modelling

Utkarsh re-analyzed data on the placebo effect and pain using computational models in MATLAB, comparing which models best explained the data. He also wrote a full study pre-registration for follow-up research. This work is being prepared for journal publication.

Computational modelling Research design

My role: I ran the computational modelling and drafted the pre-registration document for the planned follow-up study.

My experience: Learning MATLAB's VBA toolbox from scratch, and writing a pre-registration for the first time, taught me how much rigor goes into planning a study before you run it.

Research Assistantship · IIT Delhi, Department of Design

Psychometric Scale for Children's Play

Site visits — schools & homes in Delhi

Utkarsh designed and tested a new scale to measure how much young children enjoy educational play. This involved in-person site visits, testing the scale with 130 children in Delhi, and validating it using standard statistical methods.

Field visits Instrument design Child research

My role: I wrote the scale items, ran the expert reviews, and administered the study with children myself.

My experience: Working directly with young children in the field was very different from analyzing data at a desk. It taught me patience, and the value of pretesting an instrument carefully before trusting it.

Independent Project · AI in Education

Learner's AI: AI for Personalized Learning

Wanted to design a intelligent tutoring system to deliver personalised education at scale. Conducted interviews with students,understood their pain points. Identified an ideal user jounrey and crafted an information architecture

Computational modelling Research design

My role: I independently conceptualized the system and explored the technical components needed to build it, including multimodal emotion recognition, NLP-based learning content generation, Bayesian modelling of learner factors, and adaptive feedback..

My experience: I started this project out of curiosity about whether AI could move beyond simply generating educational content and instead understand how a student is learning. It pushed me to think about AI not just as a tool for automation, but as part of an adaptive learning system that responds to a learner's knowledge, engagement, difficulties, and individual needs.

Applied Research · Vibe AI

User Research for an AI Product

In-person interviews & usability sessions

Utkarsh ran in-person interviews and usability sessions with over 100 users to understand how people interact with an AI product. His findings were used directly by the product team to improve the design.

User research Product design

My role: I led the user research end to end — recruiting participants, running interviews, and presenting findings to the product team.

My experience: This was my first applied product research role. It taught me how to turn open-ended interviews into concrete, specific design recommendations.
