About

A researcher working across field, behaviour, and AI

I am a researcher working at the intersection of field research, behavioural science and Human-AI Interaction.

My research experience spans large-scale programme evaluation and public perception studies in India, experimental and computational behavioural research, and applied research on AI systems and digital learning.

At IIM Ahmedabad, I worked on large-scale government and public-policy research, including a multi-state evaluation of a Government of India rural development programme involving 6,000+ respondents and large field teams.

My current research interests centre on how AI systems can be designed, deployed and evaluated in real-world settings, particularly in education, public services and other contexts where technology interacts directly with human behaviour.

Master's Thesis · IIT Delhi

AI for Digital Learning

How can AI systems adapt learning experiences to learners' cognitive and emotional needs?

Investigated how AI could support adaptive digital learning by combining multimodal emotion recognition with LLM-based content generation.

AI Education Human-AI Interaction

Research contribution: Developed a multimodal AI pipeline combining text and audio features for emotion recognition in conversational learning contexts, addressing class imbalance through ensemble learning.

AI intervention: Fine-tuned a Gemma model to generate adaptive learning content based on learner characteristics.

Research relevance: The project sits at the intersection of AI, education, human behaviour and adaptive interaction, motivating my interest in evaluating AI systems in real-world learning environments.

My experience: This was the most technical project I've taken on. It pushed me to learn multimodal machine learning largely on my own, and to make it work end to end.

Independent Project · AI in Education

Learner's AI: AI for Personalized Learning

Wanted to design an intelligent tutoring system to deliver personalised education at scale. Conducted interviews with students, understood their pain points. Identified an ideal user journey and crafted an information architecture.

AI Education Computational Modelling

My role: I independently conceptualized the system and explored the technical components needed to build it, including multimodal emotion recognition, NLP-based learning content generation, Bayesian modelling of learner factors, and adaptive feedback.

My experience: I started this project out of curiosity about whether AI could move beyond simply generating educational content and instead understand how a student is learning. It pushed me to think about AI not just as a tool for automation, but as part of an adaptive learning system that responds to a learner's knowledge, engagement, difficulties, and individual needs.

Why Field Research + AI?

My research has increasingly focused on a common question: how do technologies and interventions change human behaviour when they move beyond controlled environments and into people's everyday lives?

My work spans large-scale field research and programme evaluation in India, behavioural and cognitive research, and Human-AI Interaction. I am particularly interested in using rigorous field methods to understand whether AI systems actually improve outcomes—and for whom—when deployed in real-world settings.
